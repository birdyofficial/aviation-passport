# Aviation Passport V0.4

Adds the live Training & Competencies module.

- Formal training records with provider, completion, expiry and evidence
- Common course suggestions plus Not Listed
- Structured competency catalogue plus Not Listed
- Optional aircraft family / variant / engine mapping
- Aircraft-specific competencies require an aircraft family
- Gained date and optional last-used date
- Edit / Remove for training and competencies
- Existing verification triggers return edited verified records to Pending
- Passport Preview now shows Training and Competencies
