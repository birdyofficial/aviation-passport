# Aviation Passport V0.5

- Adds worker-submitted Company Authorisations with employment linkage, aircraft mapping, competency mapping, dates and private evidence.
- Verified current authorisations drive the green shield.
- Editing a verified authorisation returns it to Pending and removes the shield until re-verification.
- Historical authorisations use an explicit ended date and remain in the career record.
- Passport Preview collapses complete EU-27 work rights into one European Union item while retaining all country records internally for matching.
